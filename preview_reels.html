📱 Recording Best Practices

Record vertically in 9:16 format (1080x1920) at 60fps. Stand 1-2 meters from a natural window or ring light. Use eye contact with the phone camera lens, not the screen!

🎙️ Audio & Mic Quality

Use a clip-on lavalier mic (e.g. Boya or wireless mic). Clear audio is responsible for 80% of video retention on TikTok and Reels. Add subtle trending background music at 8-12% volume.

🔤 Dynamic Captions & Text

85% of people watch reels on mute in public/office. Use apps like CapCut, AutoCap, or Premiere to generate high-contrast word-by-word animated subtitles with green/yellow highlights.

⏱️ Buffer Scheduling Timing

Best Kenyan posting windows: Morning commute (7:30 - 8:30 AM EAT), Lunch break (12:30 - 1:30 PM EAT), and Evening chill (6:30 - 8:00 PM EAT). Select Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts & LinkedIn simultaneously.
