Website pricing questions

How much does a website cost at Xclusive Tech?

The defined base builds are KES 10,000 for Express Landing Page, KES 20,000 for Business Starter, KES 35,000 for Business Growth and KES 50,000 for Online Store. Custom systems and work outside those scopes receive a written quote.

How much do I pay to start?

The build deposit is 50%: KES 5,000, KES 10,000, KES 17,500 or KES 25,000 depending on your chosen package. The balance is due after review and approval, before launch or file transfer. Annual domain and hosting charges are separate.

When does the delivery clock start?

After the deposit, complete approved content and required access arrive. Targets are 2–3 business days for the landing page, 5–7 for Business Starter, 7–10 for Business Growth and 10–15 for Online Store. Feedback delays, scope changes and merchant/provider approvals can extend delivery.

What is included and what costs extra?

Base packages include their stated page/product limits, two consolidated revision rounds, a responsive layout, foundational on-page SEO, contact form and WhatsApp. You supply approved copy and licensed images. Extra pages, copywriting, extra product uploads, extra revisions, custom APIs and paid licences are quoted separately before work starts.

Are domain, hosting and renewals included?

.co.ke registration starts at KES 1,500/year and hosting at KES 3,000/year, billed separately. The quote confirms both the first-year and renewal rate, plus any business email or paid software costs. You may use an existing suitable domain or hosting service.

Does the store price include unlimited product uploads or custom M-Pesa development?

The KES 50,000 base store includes up to 20 initial product uploads and a standard supported M-Pesa gateway setup. Larger imports, complex variations, bespoke APIs, card gateways or custom checkout work are scoped separately. Merchant approval and payment-provider charges are not part of the build price.

What happens after launch?

The package includes its stated bug-fix support period. Optional ongoing care starts at KES 2,000/month for up to four hours of updates/support. New features and work beyond that allowance receive a separate quote.
