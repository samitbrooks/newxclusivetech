How Long Does It Take to Build a Website in Kenya?

"How fast can you get this done?" is usually the second question we get, right after price. Here's a realistic, honest answer by package.

Timelines by package

  • Starter Site — about 2–3 weeks: a focused, single-purpose site covering your core pages and a clear path to contact you.
  • Business Growth — about 3–5 weeks: more pages, a content management system, and room for a blog or ongoing updates.
  • E-commerce Pro — about 5–7 weeks: product catalogues, cart and checkout flows, and payment integration, which naturally take longer to build and test properly.

These are realistic ranges, not promises padded for safety — but they assume the project starts moving without long gaps waiting on content or feedback.

What actually determines whether you hit that timeline

In practice, the single biggest factor in how long a website takes isn't the developer — it's how quickly the business supplies what's needed to build it. Projects run to schedule when:

  • Content is ready early — final text, logo, and photos, rather than "we'll send it soon" that stretches into weeks.
  • Feedback rounds are quick — a same-day or next-day response to a design draft keeps momentum; a week of silence adds a week to delivery.
  • Scope is agreed upfront — adding new pages or features mid-build is the most common source of delay on any project, not just websites.

The timelines above assume a normal pace of collaboration. A client who has content ready on day one and turns around feedback quickly can sometimes beat the low end of the range; a project that stalls waiting on photos for three weeks will simply take three weeks longer, regardless of how fast the actual build is.

Why we quote a range instead of one number

Anyone promising an exact delivery date on day one, before scope is confirmed, is either padding heavily or setting an expectation they can't reliably hit. A range is the honest version — it reflects the real variability in how projects unfold, without pretending every client's content and feedback speed will be identical.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website in Kenya?

At Xclusive Tech, a Starter Site typically takes 2–3 weeks, a Business Growth site 3–5 weeks, and an E-commerce Pro store 5–7 weeks, measured from when content and payment are confirmed, not from the first inquiry.

What slows a website project down the most?

Delayed content and feedback from the client, almost always. A developer can usually build faster than a business can supply final text, photos and approvals, so the single biggest way to speed up delivery is having your content ready before the project starts.

Can a website be built faster than the standard timeline?

Sometimes, if content is ready upfront and the scope is kept tight. But rushing a build usually means cutting corners on SEO setup, testing or design polish, which tends to cost more time later than it saves.

Does the clock start when I first make contact?

No. Delivery timelines start once the package, price and scope are agreed and a deposit is paid, and once we have (or are actively collecting) the content needed to build the site — not from the date of the first inquiry.
