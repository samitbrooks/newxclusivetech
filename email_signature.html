How to Install This Signature in 60 Seconds
Gmail / Google Workspace
  1. Click Copy Signature to Clipboard above.
  2. In Gmail, click Settings (gear) → See all settings.
  3. Scroll down to Signature, click Create new.
  4. Press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V into the signature box.
  5. Scroll to bottom and click Save Changes.
Apple Mail (Mac)
  1. Click Copy Signature to Clipboard above.
  2. Open Mail → Settings → Signatures.
  3. Click + under your account.
  4. Paste (Cmd+V) into the signature field.
  5. Uncheck "Always match my default message font".
Outlook / cPanel Webmail
  1. Click Copy Signature to Clipboard above.
  2. In cPanel Webmail (Roundcube): Settings → Identities → click your email.
  3. Toggle on HTML signature.
  4. Paste directly and click Save.
