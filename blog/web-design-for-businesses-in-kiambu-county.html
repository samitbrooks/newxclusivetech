Web Design for Businesses in Kiambu County

Nairobi is home base, but a large share of the firms we build for are in Kiambu County — Thika, Ruiru, Juja, Kikuyu, Limuru and Gatundu included. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Being close to Nairobi doesn't mean being served like Nairobi

Firms in Kiambu County sit right next to Kenya's biggest market, but that proximity doesn't automatically mean access to the same quality of web design that Nairobi businesses take for granted. A lot of agencies quote Nairobi clients properly and treat everyone else as an afterthought — slower replies, vague pricing, or simply not bothering to understand that a business in Thika or Ruiru has the same standards as one in Westlands. We don't do that. Kiambu County firms get the same published pricing, the same delivery timelines, and the same direct WhatsApp access as anyone else on our books.

Where we build

Alongside Kiambu town itself, we regularly build for businesses across the county, including:

  • Thika — manufacturers, retailers and service businesses along the Thika Superhighway corridor.
  • Ruiru — a fast-growing town with a mix of new businesses that need to establish themselves online quickly.
  • Juja — businesses serving a large student and young professional population.
  • Kikuyu and Limuru — established local businesses looking to reach beyond word of mouth.
  • Gatundu and Githunguri — agribusiness, retail and professional services reaching both local and Nairobi-based customers.

Transparent pricing, wherever you're based

The same published packages apply across Kiambu County as everywhere else we work:

  • Starter Site — KES 31,500–45,500: a focused site with your core pages and a clear path to contact you, live in about 2–3 weeks.
  • Business Growth — KES 49,000–62,000: more pages, a content management system, and room for a blog.
  • E-commerce Pro — KES 72,000–85,000: a full online store with M-Pesa (Daraja API) checkout built in.
  • Custom Solutions — from KES 85,000: web applications, booking systems and custom integrations.

No "call for a quote" pricing that changes depending on who's asking. What's published is what you pay, with room to tailor the scope to your budget.

Local visibility matters as much as the website itself

A website for a Kiambu County business needs to actually show up when someone searches for that service locally — "web design Kiambu," "contractor in Ruiru," or similar. Every website we build includes basic on-page SEO, and we set up and optimise a Google Business Profile so local searches lead somewhere real, not just to a generic national listing.
