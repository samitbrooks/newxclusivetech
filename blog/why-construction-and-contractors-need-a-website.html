Why Construction & Contracting Companies in Kenya Need a Website

Referrals get a contractor in the room. A website is what a client checks before they hand over a deposit on a project worth millions.

Big projects mean big due diligence

Construction is a high-stakes, high-trust decision. Before a client commits to a contractor for a home, a commercial building, or a renovation, they want proof: completed projects, the scale of work you actually handle, and evidence you'll still be reachable if something goes wrong months into the build. Word of mouth might get you the first conversation, but a client will still search your company name before they sign anything — and finding nothing, or an outdated Facebook page, raises exactly the doubt you don't want at that stage.

What a construction company website actually needs

  • A real project portfolio — photos of completed work, organised by project type (residential, commercial, renovation), not one long unsorted gallery.
  • Clear service scope — what you handle end-to-end versus what's subcontracted, so expectations are set before the first site visit.
  • Credentials that matter — NCA registration, certifications, and years in business, placed where a cautious client will actually look.
  • An easy way to request a quote — a form asking for project type and scale, so the first conversation starts with useful information instead of "what do you guys do?"
  • Mobile-first design — a client is often browsing your portfolio from a site visit or while comparing contractors on the go.

Which package fits a construction or contracting business

A Business Growth package tends to fit best — enough pages to properly categorise a growing project portfolio, plus a CMS to add completed projects as they wrap up, without going back to a developer every time. Smaller contractors or specialist trades with a lighter portfolio can start with a Starter Site and expand later.

Showing up when it matters most

Most construction searches are local and specific — "contractor in Kiambu," "commercial builder Nairobi" — which makes local SEO especially valuable in this industry. A Google Business Profile linked to a real website, with consistent contact details and genuine project photos, is what turns a search into a shortlisted call.
